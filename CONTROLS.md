# Arman CC Camera Scanner - Controls & Shortcuts

Here are the keyboard shortcuts and operational controls for managing the scanner smoothly:

## ⌨️ Keyboard Shortcuts (Terminal Controls)
- **`Ctrl + C`** : Stops the ongoing scan immediately and exits the tool safely.
- **`Ctrl + Z`** : Pauses the active scan. Press `Ctrl + Z` again to resume it right where it left off.

## 🎛️ Menu Guide
1. **Update IP Ranges**: Downloads and updates the fresh Bangladesh IPv4 ranges directly from APNIC.
2. **Scan Cameras**: Starts multi-threaded scanning across the IP ranges to detect active CCTV interfaces.
3. **Exit**: Closes the application.
